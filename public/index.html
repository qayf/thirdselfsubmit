Submissions / Open call

Someone has to write it down.

The Third Self Project publishes essays, stories, and field notes about how digital environments shape what we desire, how we behave, and who we become. If you have been paying attention, we want to read it.

Reading period
Rolling — always open
Response time
4–6 weeks
Length
800–5,000 words
Rights
First publication, then yours

Fig. 02Before you send

What we’re looking for

  • Writing rooted in something you actually observed — in yourself, in a room, in a feed.
  • A piece that fits one of our five issues, or argues for a sixth.
  • Finished drafts. We read the work, not the pitch.
  • Any form: essay, story, reported piece, poem, or a short field note.
